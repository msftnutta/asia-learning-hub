Frequently Asked Questions

Still have doubts? Visit our Help Center or chat with our team 24/7.

Are the promotional rates guaranteed?

Yes — once you lock in a fixed-term deposit or fixed-rate loan, your rate is contractually guaranteed for the full term, even if market rates change. Promotional rates apply to new accounts opened before April 30, 2026.

Is my deposit money safe?

Absolutely. All deposits are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 through our partner bank. High-yield deposits are further ring-fenced from operational capital. Bonds are held in your name via a regulated custodian.

Can I break a fixed deposit early?

Yes, early exits are allowed after a 30-day cooling-off period. You keep all interest accrued to date, minus a 0.5% early exit fee. No punitive penalties — we believe your money should stay accessible.

What credit score do I need for a personal loan?

We use a holistic assessment — not just credit score. Our AI model considers income stability, spending behaviour, and credit history. Many customers with scores of 600+ are approved. Checking your eligibility is a soft inquiry and won't affect your score.

How do bond investments work on Contoso Bank?

You purchase bonds directly via the app in fractional units from $100. Coupon payments are deposited into your Contoso Bank wallet on schedule. You can sell on our secondary marketplace before maturity at market price.

How fast is mortgage pre-approval?

Most applicants receive a pre-approval decision within 24 hours. We use open banking data and automated underwriting to assess your application instantly — no mountains of paperwork required.
