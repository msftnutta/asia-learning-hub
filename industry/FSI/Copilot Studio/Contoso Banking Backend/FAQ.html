Getting Started

Everything you need to open and use your Contoso Bank account

Opening a Contoso Bank account takes less than 5 minutes. Download the Contoso Bank app from the App Store or Google Play, then:

  1. Tap Create Account and enter your email address.
  2. Verify your email with the 6-digit code we send you.
  3. Complete a quick identity verification by scanning your government-issued ID and taking a selfie.
  4. Set up your PIN and biometric authentication.
  5. Your account is instantly active — no waiting period required.
Your account is FDIC-insured up to $250,000 through our partner bank.

You'll need the following to verify your identity (KYC compliance):

  • Primary ID: Passport, national ID card, or driver's license
  • Proof of address: Utility bill, bank statement, or lease (dated within 90 days)
  • Selfie: A clear, real-time photo taken during onboarding

All documents must be valid (not expired) and clearly legible.

Contoso Bank currently serves customers in 42 countries across North America, Europe, Southeast Asia, and Oceania. You can check eligibility by entering your country on our availability page.

We're expanding rapidly. Join our waitlist if your country isn't listed yet.

Deposits

Fund your account quickly and securely

You can add money to your account through several methods:

  • Bank transfer (ACH): Link an external bank — arrives in 1–3 business days (free)
  • Instant bank transfer: Via supported banks — arrives within minutes ($0.99 fee)
  • Debit card top-up: Immediate — up to $1,000/day (1% fee, minimum $0.50)
  • Direct deposit: Set Contoso Bank as your payroll destination — free and instant on payday
  • Cash deposit: At 60,000+ partner retail locations (fees may apply)
  • Check deposit: Mobile photo scan in-app — 1–2 business day hold

Processing times vary by method:

  • Standard ACH: 1–3 business days
  • Same-day ACH: Same business day if submitted before 3 PM ET (additional fee)
  • Instant transfer: Within minutes (supported banks only)
  • Debit card top-up: Immediate
  • Wire transfer: 1 business day (domestic), 2–5 days (international)
  • Check mobile deposit: Next business day for amounts up to $500; 2 days for larger amounts
First-time deposits may have an additional 1-day hold for fraud prevention.

Mobile check deposit is easy. Follow these steps:

  1. In the app, go to Move Money → Deposit Check.
  2. Enter the check amount.
  3. Place the check on a flat, well-lit surface.
  4. Photograph the front of the check — make sure all four corners are visible.
  5. Photograph the back of the check — endorse it first with your signature and "For Contoso Bank mobile deposit only".
  6. Review and submit.
Keep the physical check for 30 days after it clears, then shred it.

Direct deposit is the fastest and most reliable way to fund your account. To set it up:

  1. Go to Move Money → Direct Deposit.
  2. Your routing number and account number are displayed — share these with your employer's payroll department.
  3. Alternatively, tap Download Pre-filled Form to get a ready-to-submit PDF.

Contoso Bank offers early paycheck access — get your direct deposit up to 2 days early.

Withdrawals

Access your money whenever you need it

There are multiple ways to access your funds:

  • ATM withdrawal: Use your Contoso Bank debit card at any ATM in our network
  • Transfer to external bank: Via ACH or wire transfer
  • Cash back at retail: Get cash back at participating stores when you make a purchase
  • Instant transfer: Transfer to an eligible debit card instantly

Contoso Bank has over 55,000 fee-free ATMs nationwide through the Allpoint and MoneyPass networks. To find one:

  1. Open the app and go to Cards → Find ATM.
  2. Use your current location or enter an address.
  3. Filter by "Fee-free only" to see Contoso Bank network ATMs.

You can also search via the Allpoint or MoneyPass ATM locator on their websites.

ATM fee structure by account tier:

  • Starter: First 2 withdrawals/month free, then $2.50/withdrawal at Contoso Bank-network ATMs; $3.50 at out-of-network ATMs
  • Plus: First 5 withdrawals/month free, then $2.50/withdrawal; $3.50 out-of-network
  • Premium: Unlimited free withdrawals, $2.50 reimbursed for out-of-network (up to $30/month)
Third-party ATM operators may charge their own surcharge, which we display before you confirm.

Default ATM withdrawal limits:

  • Per transaction: $500
  • Per day: $1,000 (Starter/Plus), $2,500 (Premium)
  • Per month: $5,000 (Starter), $10,000 (Plus), $25,000 (Premium)

Note that the ATM itself may have lower limits. You can request a temporary limit increase in-app under Cards → Manage Limits.

To transfer money out to an external bank:

  1. Go to Move Money → Transfer Out.
  2. Select an already-linked external account, or link a new one by entering routing and account numbers.
  3. Enter the amount and choose Standard (free, 1–3 days) or Instant (fee applies).
  4. Confirm with your PIN or biometrics.

Common reasons for a declined withdrawal:

  • Insufficient funds — your available balance is less than the withdrawal amount
  • Daily/monthly limit reached — you've hit your withdrawal cap
  • Card frozen or blocked — check Cards → Manage Card
  • ATM out of cash — try a different machine
  • Suspicious activity hold — an automated security hold was placed on your account
  • International block — overseas spending may be blocked if not enabled in settings

If the issue persists, contact support via live chat immediately.

Yes! Cash back is available at most grocery stores, pharmacies, and big-box retailers when you select Debit as the payment method and enter your PIN. This counts toward your daily ATM withdrawal limit.

Transfers

Send money to anyone, anywhere

Sending money to a fellow Contoso Bank user is instant and free:

  1. Tap Move Money → Send.
  2. Search by name, @username, phone number, or email.
  3. Enter the amount and an optional note.
  4. Tap Send and confirm with PIN or biometrics.

The money arrives in the recipient's account within seconds, 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

To send an ACH or wire transfer:

  1. Go to Move Money → Bank Transfer.
  2. Add the recipient's bank routing number and account number.
  3. Choose ACH (free, 1–3 business days) or Wire ($15 fee, same day if before 3 PM ET).
  4. Review and confirm.
Double-check account numbers carefully — misdirected wire transfers are difficult to reverse.

  • Weekly

The ability to cancel depends on the transfer type and status:

  • Scheduled future transfer: Fully cancellable before the execution date via Move Money → Scheduled.
  • ACH (pending): Cancellable within 30 minutes of submission.
  • Wire (pending): Cancellable before the cut-off time (3 PM ET); $25 recall fee thereafter.

To request money:

  1. Tap Move Money → Request.
  2. Select a contact or enter their Contoso Bank username/phone/email.
  3. Enter the amount and add an optional description (e.g., "Dinner last night").
  4. Tap Request. The recipient gets a push notification to approve and pay instantly.

You can also use payment links — share your personal contoso-bank.io/pay/@yourname link for quick payments from anyone.

Contoso Bank's built-in split feature makes splitting expenses easy:

  1. Tap any transaction and select Split this.
  2. Add Contoso Bank contacts to split with.
  3. Choose equal split or enter custom amounts for each person.
  4. Send requests with one tap. Everyone gets notified instantly.

You can also create a group pool for ongoing shared expenses (e.g., roommates, trips).

Overseas Transactions

Spend and send money globally with confidence

Yes! Your Contoso Bank Visa debit card is accepted at over 84 million merchant locations in more than 200 countries and territories. Before traveling:

  1. Enable international spending: Cards → Manage Card → International Spending
  2. Optionally set the countries or regions you'll visit for enhanced security
  3. Ensure your phone number is up to date for verification purposes
Let us know your travel dates in the app for seamless, uninterrupted spending abroad.

Contoso Bank always uses the real mid-market interbank rate — no hidden markup is added to the exchange rate itself.

To send money internationally:

  1. Go to Move Money → International Transfer.
  2. Select the destination country and currency.
  3. Enter the recipient's name, IBAN (for SEPA/EU) or account number + SWIFT/BIC code.
  4. Review the real-time exchange rate and total fees before confirming.
  5. Large transfers may require additional documentation per regulatory requirements.

Transfers to 35+ countries arrive within 1 business day. Standard to all other countries is 2–5 business days.

Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) is when a foreign merchant or ATM offers to charge you in your home currency instead of the local one. We strongly recommend declining DCC every time, because:

  • Merchants apply their own (often very poor) exchange rate
  • DCC typically adds a 3–7% surcharge compared to the interbank rate
  • Contoso Bank always applies the interbank rate — letting us handle the conversion saves you money
Always choose to be charged in the local currency. Contoso Bank will handle the conversion.

Overseas ATM fees depend on your account tier:

  • Starter: $3.00/withdrawal + 1.5% FX fee
  • Plus: First 2 international withdrawals/month free; then $3.00 + 0.5% FX fee
  • Premium: First 10 international withdrawals/month free; then $3.00 (FX fee waived)

Note: Local ATM operators in some countries may add their own fees, which are disclosed before you proceed.

If your card is declined overseas:

  1. Check the app — there may be an alert explaining the reason (suspicious transaction, limit reached, country not enabled, etc.).
  2. Go to Cards → Manage Card → International Spending and verify the country is enabled.
  3. If the card appears frozen, tap Unfreeze Card.
  4. Contact live support via in-app chat — our international team is available 24/7.
Save our emergency number +1-800-NEO-HELP in your phone before you travel.

Plus and Premium members can hold balances in up to 15 currencies simultaneously in their Contoso Bank wallet. Supported currencies include USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, CAD, CHF, SGD, HKD, THB, MYR, INR, KRW, BRL, and MXN.

To add a currency wallet:

  1. Go to Cards → Currency Wallets → Add Wallet.
  2. Select your desired currency.
  3. Fund it from your main balance at the real interbank rate.

When spending abroad, Contoso Bank automatically uses the matching currency wallet if available — eliminating FX fees entirely for those transactions.

Security

Keeping your money and data safe

Your money and data are protected by multiple layers of security:

  • FDIC Insurance: Deposits insured up to $250,000 per depositor through our partner bank
  • 256-bit AES encryption: All data encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Real-time fraud monitoring: AI models review every transaction 24/7
  • Zero-liability fraud protection: You're not responsible for unauthorized transactions reported promptly
  • Biometric authentication: Face ID / fingerprint required for sensitive actions
  • 3D Secure: All online card transactions use 3D Secure 2.0 verification

Act immediately if you suspect unauthorized access:

  1. Freeze your card immediately via Cards → Freeze Card (takes effect in seconds).
  2. Change your password via Profile → Security → Change Password.
  3. Revoke all active sessions under Profile → Security → Active Sessions → Sign out all devices.
  4. Contact support immediately via in-app chat or +1-800-NEO-HELP.
  5. File a dispute for any unauthorized transactions you identify.
Never share your PIN, password, or one-time codes with anyone — including people claiming to be Contoso Bank staff.

2FA is automatically enabled for all Contoso Bank accounts. You can choose your second factor:

  • SMS OTP — 6-digit code sent to your registered phone number
  • Authenticator app — TOTP via Google Authenticator, Authy, or compatible apps
  • Hardware security key — FIDO2/WebAuthn support (recommended for highest security)

To upgrade your 2FA method: Profile → Security → Two-Factor Authentication.

To dispute a transaction:

  1. Tap the transaction in your transaction history.
  2. Scroll down and tap Report a Problem.
  3. Select the reason: I didn't make this, Wrong amount charged, Item not received, etc.
  4. Add any supporting details and submit.

We'll issue a provisional credit within 10 business days while we investigate. Most disputes are resolved within 5 business days.

You can also granularly control what your card can be used for:

  • Online transactions

We actively protect you in several ways:

  • Scam warnings: The app flags transfers to new recipients with "first transfer" alerts
  • Merchant verification: We display merchant names, logos, and locations for every transaction
  • No-callback policy: We will never call you and ask for your full card number, PIN, or one-time password
  • Secure messaging: All legitimate Contoso Bank communications come through the in-app message center — not external SMS

If you receive a suspicious call, email, or message claiming to be from Contoso Bank, forward it to security@contoso-bank.io.

Account Management

Manage your profile, preferences, and account settings

To update your information, go to Profile → Personal Info:

  • Name: Requires government ID upload for legal name changes
  • Email address: Instant update with OTP verification on both old and new email
  • Phone number: Immediate with OTP; a 24-hour security lock follows
  • Home address: Update with proof-of-address document upload
  • Profile picture: Change anytime with no verification required

You can change your plan at any time:

  • To upgrade: Profile → Plan → Upgrade — new benefits take effect immediately; billing is prorated.
  • To downgrade: Profile → Plan → Downgrade — the change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle; you keep current benefits until then.

If you hold currency wallets or features exclusive to your current plan, we'll help you migrate them before the downgrade takes effect.

We're sorry to see you go. To close your account:

  1. Ensure your balance is $0.00 or transfer remaining funds to an external account.
  2. Cancel any active subscriptions or recurring payments linked to your card.
  3. Go to Profile → Account → Close Account.
  4. Complete the identity verification step.
  5. Your account will be closed within 5 business days.

You'll receive a final statement via email. We retain records for 7 years as required by law, though your data will not be used for marketing.
